Monkey Path privacy policy

This page is a work in progress. It describes the closed alpha and follows the game at every change. The complete version (retention periods, rights, review) will be published before the beta.

Alpha version, updated 6 October 2026.

In short

  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone. There are no accounts in the alpha.
  • The game only keeps an alpha log if you accept. Declining changes nothing in the game.
  • Nothing leaves your phone automatically. The log only leaves if you export it and send it to the team yourself.
  • No ads, no data resale, no advertising ID.

Who is the alpha for?

The alpha is closed. It is open to testers aged 15 and over, which each tester confirms when signing up.

What stays on your phone

Your saved progress (level, items, talents, zone progress) and your settings (language, sound, accessibility, loot filter) are written to the game's private storage on your phone. They are not sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game erases them.

The alpha log (optional)

On first launch, the game asks whether you accept that it keeps a log to make the alpha more reliable and better balanced. Both answers carry the same weight. You can change your mind at any time in Options → Usage statistics.

If you accept, the game draws a random installation ID (it does not come from your nickname, your e-mail, or a Google or Apple account) and records, by family:

Each line of the log also carries the time, the session number, the game version and the installation ID.

Never collected: content of mottos and nicknames, friends list, location, contacts, advertising ID, phone identifier (IMEI, Android ID), list of installed apps, screenshots, text you type.

If you decline (or withdraw your consent later), the installation ID and the log are erased.

Where the log goes

The game writes the log to a file on your phone, with a size cap, and sends nothing: there is no sending tool and no server.

Manual export. The Options → Usage statistics → Export the log button produces a single file, monkeypath-rapport.txt, which gathers the alpha log and the error log as they are when you tap it. Its header carries the date, the game version, the installation ID and the phone model. You are the one who sends this file to the team, in the alpha's feedback channel (Discord or WhatsApp), by the end of the alpha at the latest.

Deletion. The team reads the logs it receives and deletes them at the end of the alpha. On your phone, the Clear the log button in the options erases the log whenever you want.

If the team later chooses a sending tool, this page will be updated before the game sends anything, naming the service, its hosting country and the retention period.

Error log

The game sends no crash reports. It records its errors in a log on your phone; this log only leaves your phone if you export it yourself, with the Export the log button, and send the file to the team.

Giving feedback

The "Give feedback" button, when present, opens the alpha's feedback channel (a Discord server). You can also message the team on WhatsApp. What you write there, and your username on that channel, are subject to the rules of the service hosting it.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or erasure, object to its processing or withdraw your consent, by writing to the address above. You can also contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Deleting your data

The alpha has no accounts: your progress is local, and uninstalling the game erases it. To erase the installation ID and the log without uninstalling, decline usage statistics in the options. The logs you sent to the team are deleted at the end of the alpha. When accounts exist, this page will explain how to delete them.

Changes

This page is updated when the game changes what it records: a new family of data is described here before it is recorded. The date at the top shows the latest version.
