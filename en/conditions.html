An alpha, as is

Monkey Path is in closed alpha on Android: a test version, given as is to a small group of testers. It is not published on Google Play: the APK is installed from a link, and is updated by installing the new version over the old one.

Progress is kept on your phone, without an account: uninstalling the game erases it.

Who can play

The alpha is open to testers aged 15 and over, which each tester confirms when signing up.

Your feedback

The alpha exists to gather your feedback. You give it in the alpha's feedback channel (Discord or WhatsApp), which the "Give feedback" button opens when it is present. If you accepted the alpha log, you can also export it and send it to the team yourself: the privacy policy says what it contains.
