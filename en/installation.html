Installing the Monkey Path alpha

Alpha version, updated 6 October 2026.

What the alpha is

Monkey Path is in closed alpha on Android: a test version, given as is to a small group of testers aged 15 and over. It is not on Google Play: you install the APK file by hand, from a link. The terms of use say what that means.

1. Download the APK

Download the latest version

Or, on your Android phone, open the releases page: https://github.com/JamesRr06/MonkeyPath-releases/releases. Pick the latest version and download the file monkey-path-release.apk.

Only download the APK from this page: the link was given to you when you signed up, and nobody but the team publishes it.

2. Allow the installation

By default, Android blocks apps that do not come from a store. When you open the APK, your phone tells you that you must allow installing from this source (your browser or your file manager): follow the link it offers to its settings, then allow this source. Menu names change with the Android version and the phone brand; if you cannot find it, search the settings for "unknown sources" or "install unknown apps".

You can remove this permission after the installation.

3. Install

Open the downloaded file, then confirm the installation. Then start Monkey Path. On first launch, the game asks whether you accept the alpha log: you may decline, and the game stays the same.

4. Update

To move to a new version, download its APK and install it over the old one, without uninstalling anything: your progress is kept. If Android refuses the update, do not uninstall the game: write to the team first, because uninstalling erases your progress.

5. Send your feedback

Give your feedback in the alpha's feedback channel (Discord or WhatsApp) that was given to you when you signed up. If you accepted the alpha log, also send its report: in the game, open Options, then Usage statistics, then Export the log, in a single click. You get one file, which you pass on as it is in Discord or WhatsApp, without editing it. A new click makes a new report, which replaces the previous one. Nothing is sent on its own: the privacy policy says what the log contains.

6. Uninstall

To leave the alpha, uninstall the game like any other app. This erases your progress: it is kept on your phone, without an account, and nobody can give it back to you. The logs received by the team are deleted at the end of the Alpha release.
